A Letter to You

Dear Mom,

I know I don't say it enough, but as I grow older, I realize more and more the sheer magnitude of what you've done for me. You carried the weight of the world so that I could learn how to fly.

You have been my steady ground, my fiercest protector, and the quiet force behind every good thing in my life. The sacrifices you made—the sleep you lost, the dreams you postponed, the invisible burdens you carried—none of it was in vain. I see it all now. I see you.

Thank you for giving me the world, even when yours was heavy. Today, I want you to simply rest, breathe, and know how deeply you are loved, appreciated, and cherished.

With all my love,
Your Son
