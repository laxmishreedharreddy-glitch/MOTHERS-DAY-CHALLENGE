## Objective

Build a website dedicated to your mother — something that would genuinely impress her.

This is not just a technical exercise. The site must carry **emotion, effort, and craft**. It should make a mother feel celebrated.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Ratings are given **out of 10** by fellow Kohei.

### How voting works

- **All students vote** on each demo
- **Exception:** Team members **cannot vote** for their own teammates
- Ratings are **individual** — your score belongs to you and your team

### What to evaluate

| Criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| **Emotional Impact** | Does it feel personal and heartfelt? |
| **Design & Aesthetics** | Is it visually polished and thoughtful? |
| **Technical Execution** | Is the site functional, responsive, well-built? |
| **Originality** | Is the concept unique and creative? |
| **Presentation** | Was the demo clear, confident, and engaging? |

---

## Rules

1. You **cannot rate** anyone from your own team
2. No last-minute plagiarism — all work must be your own
3. The site must be **original** — built for this challenge, not recycled
4. Be respectful during other members' demos (no interruptions)
